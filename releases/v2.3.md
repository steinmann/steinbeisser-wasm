# Steinbeisser 2.3

## Changes

- Replaced the 2.2 NNUE model with `d09-11-lr125`, selected through a larger model-training campaign. This candidate used a learning rate of `1.25e-4` instead of the `1.0e-4` control rate.
- The candidate used the otherwise unchanged Round 9 training recipe.
- Search, move generation, time management, NNUE architecture, and runtime are unchanged.
